title: "Vorticity: watching a migration"
weight: 3
description: "Capital as a conserved fluid. Rivers versus vortices, why panic is not a flow, and what the current reading of 0.84 and falling means."
---

Inside a closed basket, capital share is zero-sum. Track the daily change in each sector's share of the basket and you have a flow field: where weight is moving from and to. Compute the lead–lag structure between sectors — does Tech's gain today predict Energy's gain tomorrow? — and you get an antisymmetric matrix that can be decomposed, Hodge-style, into two parts. The gradient part is *directed* flow: a river, with an upstream and a downstream. The curl part is *circulation*: a vortex, weight going round and round with no net destination.

**Vorticity** is the curl share of the total. Close to 1 means the field is almost pure rotation — capital sloshing between sectors with no direction. Lower values mean a river has formed.

Three findings from the research era shape how the dial is read:

**The field is mostly vortex.** In the research era, most of the flow was circulation; directed rivers were the exception, and that is what makes them informative. (A caveat we owe you: even random lead–lag noise decomposes as mostly curl, and we have not yet published a shuffled-returns null for this dial. It is on the quarterly-review list.)

**Panic is not a flow.** In crashes the lead–lag structure dissolves rather than organizing; flight-to-safety accounts for only two or three percentage points of share. Fear is not capital running somewhere. It is capital losing its sense of direction.

**Rivers form at transitions.** Mid-2006. The vaccine rotation of late 2020, among the most directed fields we measured. And 2026: Financial and Tech upstream, Consumer and Health downstream, with net migration of about three points into Tech, two into Energy, and three and a half out of Consumer since the panel began in December 2025.

The current reading — 0.836, declining — is consistent with a migration still underway. The tripwire T1 fires when vorticity crosses back above 0.92: the river has finished cutting its channel and the field is dissolving back into rotation. That is not a crash signal. It is a *the move you were watching is over* reading, which is a different and in some ways more interesting thing.

The combined read we are betting on: **calm space plus a forming river looks like a regime transition, not a crisis.** Wide dimension suggests a healthy body; a directed flow suggests it is rearranging. Every claim in the paper experiment is downstream of this read, which is how it gets tested. That is the state the Observatory was born into, and it is the state the paper experiment is designed to test.

(A slime mold, when it rearranges, does exactly this: broad circulating flow condenses into a few directed veins toward the new food. The organism model is a working metaphor — it earns its keep only as long as the measurements it frames keep grading well.)

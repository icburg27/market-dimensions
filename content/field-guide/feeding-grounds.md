## Meals and scent

In the organism model, a meal is cash flow and scent is expected earnings. Growth happens where the organism is feeding; die-back happens where there is scent without meal (the dot-com pattern), where a patch is depleted, or where flow reroutes around a vessel (private credit around bank arteries). The **artery test** for any stock is simple: do other organisms' meals have to pass through it? JPMorgan, Visa, ASML pass. Most companies do not.

## The frontier detector

The AI pocket — NVDA and AMD — is where the organism is currently feeding. The frontier detector asks which other stocks are starting to move with the pocket rather than with the crowd. Whether that co-movement runs ahead of relative return is the claim under test — not the premise; a correlation computed from prices cannot literally see before prices do. Score = trailing-60-day correlation to the pocket minus mean correlation to the core-20 body. A high score means a stock has started moving with the growth tip and not with the crowd.

The current live ranking, straight from `latest.json`: TSM 0.885, VRT 0.834, AVGO 0.728, ETN 0.718, ANET 0.698 — all outscoring NVDA (0.66) and AMD (0.63) themselves. On the detector, the frontier appears to have spread into the supply seam: the foundry, the cooling, the networking, the power gear. Two connected names, VST and CEG, scored well on the detector but had been pruned by price (down 14–21% on the year on the day the paper experiment opened), which is exactly the kind of disagreement the paper experiment exists to resolve — and exactly the one that, eight days in, is running against us.

The detector is a fair-weather instrument. In a clench, every score compresses toward zero because everything correlates with everything. Read it in sunlight.

## The 356.69 pair

On 2026-08-25, Broadcom and JPMorgan both closed at $356.69. A feeding-ground stock and an artery, at parity by accident. The identical price is a coincidence with no information in it — but it was too tidy not to register something against, so we registered two claims: PAIR-1, that the ratio AVGO/JPM rises while the sun is out (dimension high, vorticity below 0.92); and PAIR-2, that in any clench (dimension below 4) the ratio falls, because plumbing outperforms frontier in a panic. The ratio is a standing dial on the Observatory strip. It is currently 0.998 — a hair under parity, against PAIR-1's direction, and on the ledger. *The pair, like everything in the experiment, is paper: a registered claim, not a position. And PAIR-2, if it grades correct, is mostly textbook low-beta behavior — it would not by itself be evidence for the organism model.*

A magician who shows you the trick going wrong should also show you where the trick could *be* wrong. These are the objections we would raise if we were reading this site for the first time with a quantitative background and a raised eyebrow. Some we can answer. Some we can only owe.

## The August 2007 "warning" was the quant quake

True. The dimension drop in the first week of August 2007 coincides with BNP Paribas freezing three funds and the Fed cutting the discount rate — front-page credit stress, not a whisper only the geometry heard. The geometry does not predict the quant quake; it *is* the quant quake. The only thing it adds is what happened next: structure stayed compressed while prices made new highs into October. That narrower claim is the one we stand on. Where our copy has ever said "nobody saw it coming," it was wrong, and we have fixed it.

## T2 was written after looking at 2007

Also true, which means 2007 cannot count as evidence for it. The disease-signature rule has one confirming case and no published record on the other drawdowns in the panel — 2011, 2015–16, late 2018, 2022. That table of hits and false alarms is the honest test, and we owe it to you. Until it exists, treat T2 as a hypothesis with a tripwire attached, not a track record.

## Twenty stocks and sixty days is a noisy estimator

Effective dimension here comes from a 20×20 correlation matrix on 60 daily returns. Random-matrix theory says a meaningful share of the eigenvalue spread at that ratio is sampling noise, so a move of a dimension or so week to week can be nothing at all. We report point estimates. We have not yet published bootstrap bands, and we should. Related: readings are stepped every three sessions, so consecutive readings share about 95% of their data — "three consecutive readings under the line" is closer to one observation than three, and part of every slow recovery is the sixty-day window taking sixty days to forget.

## The historical panel is a panel of survivors

Twenty names that existed for the whole of 1990–2022 are, by construction, the ones that survived. The 2008 replay contains no bank that failed — no Lehman, no Bear, no Wachovia, no WaMu. A panel that included the casualties would look different, probably worse, and we do not know by how much.

## The live basket and the historical panel are different stocks

Effective dimension is not basket-invariant; it depends on composition and sector balance. So "wider than 2006" is a loose comparison, not a measurement. The only clean comparisons on this site are live-against-live, which is why the history of readings starts in 2026 and why the replays are labeled as a different panel drawn at the same scale.

## The picture is a projection

The constellation is a two-dimensional shadow of a shape that lives in ten or more dimensions, and the projection is at its least faithful precisely when the cloud is widest and prettiest. Two stars can look close on screen and be far apart in the full metric. The numbers on the strip are computed in the full space; the picture is not. Treat on-screen distances as suggestive.

## The paper experiment is one window in one regime

Nine claims, six months, a single market state. Most of the claims are directional, so roughly half would grade correct by chance; six of nine is not evidence, and nine of nine is only mild evidence. The experiment becomes meaningful across several regimes. We are in the first one.

## The frontier detector is correlation, which is prices

The detector is built from trailing correlation to the AI pocket, and correlation is computed from prices — so it cannot literally see "before prices do." What it might do is flag co-movement before *relative return* diverges. That is indistinguishable from thematic beta or momentum unless it beats a momentum baseline, and we have not run that comparison.

## The 356.69 pair is numerology

Two stocks sharing a closing price carries no information; share counts are arbitrary. We kept it because it made a memorable dial. The real claim underneath is only "frontier beats artery in sunlight; artery beats frontier in a clench," and the second half is textbook low-beta behavior, not geometry. PAIR-2 grading correct would not be evidence for the organism model.

## Vorticity has no published null

The flow dial comes from a six-sector lead–lag matrix, and even pure noise decomposes as mostly curl. We have not yet published a shuffled-returns baseline, so we cannot say how much of "rivers are rare" is market structure and how much is arithmetic.

## Every threshold was chosen with the history in view

0.35 for a strand, 0.92 for vorticity, 75% of trailing max for T2, 1.4 for separation, dimension below 4 for a clench, sixty days for the window. None of them is derived; all of them were picked while looking at the same charts we then show you. The only cure we know is to freeze them and grade forward, which is why metric definitions change only at quarterly review and why nothing graded before the freeze counts.

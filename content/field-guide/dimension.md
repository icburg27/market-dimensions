Take the correlation matrix of the twenty stocks and compute its eigenvalues. If every stock moved independently, all twenty eigenvalues would be equal and the cloud would fill twenty dimensions. If every stock moved identically, one eigenvalue would hold everything and the cloud would be a line. Effective dimension is the participation ratio, (Σλ)² / Σλ², which counts how many directions are *actually* in use. It is the number of independent things the market is doing at once.

**Levels, roughly.** Nine to ten is wide — a differentiated body with room to move. Seven to nine is healthy. Five to seven is narrowing and worth watching. Three to five is the 2007 territory. Two to three is a huddle, the market moving as a single object.

**Why it is the headline.** Because diversification is not a portfolio property; it is a geometric one. It exists only while the cloud has volume. By late 2008 the effective dimension of our twenty-stock panel was two — which, if the measure means what we think, made owning twenty stocks very nearly owning one. There was nowhere orthogonal left to stand. "Diversified" portfolios fell together not because their managers failed but because the space they lived in had contracted to a line.

**Why the trajectory matters more than the level.** Dimension can fall two ways. An exogenous shock — COVID — collapses it in days, from seven to under two in sixteen trading days, and it re-inflates without leaving a scar: an injury. An endogenous rot — 2007 — decays it slowly, in stages, and each stage fails to recover: a disease. The number at the bottom is the same. The path is what we think distinguishes them, and the path is what the T2 tripwire watches.

**The ratchet.** Fear compresses the space instantly; trust re-expands it one dimension at a time. Across every crash we measured, the price recovered many times faster than the structure — in 2020 the price half-life was about three weeks and the dimension half-life about five and a half months. The market's shield has gotten faster (policy reflex, clotting speed), but the internal compression floor has not risen: every episode still bottoms near dimension two. The shield is not armor. It is the speed of the response.

The gauge above breathes with the current reading. Its amplitude is the room. Scrub back through the history and watch it shallow in late June 2026 (dimension 7.9, a wobble) and fill back out through July and August. The dashed amber ring is the T2 line: 75% of the trailing maximum. Three consecutive readings under it and the machine opens an issue. (One honest footnote: the trailing maximum needs forty readings to exist, and the machine has thirty-two, so T2 is armed but cannot fire until late October 2026.)

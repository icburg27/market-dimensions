Two collapses to dimension two, with nothing else in common. This page is the case for reading trajectory rather than level.

## The disease

Through 2006 the twenty-asset panel lives in seven to nine dimensions, and by February 2007 it is above ten. Then it breaks in two legs. The first, in the spring, takes it from above 10 to about 4 in April, from which it only half recovers. The second comes in the first week of August 2007 — the quant quake, when a French bank froze three funds and credit stress first surfaced in equities — and takes it from roughly 6 to 3.6 in a month. It never recovers. Through September and October, while the Dow makes an all-time high, dimension sits between 3.0 and 3.3; it ends the year near 4. The surface said *fine*. In hindsight, the shape had already folded. Whether that could have been read in advance is the open question this site exists to test — and to be exact about what the geometry adds: the August drop *is* the quant quake, which was front-page news. What the quant quake did not tell you is that the structure would stay compressed while prices made new highs.

Thirteen months later, Lehman starts the last leg: from 4.3 in mid-September to 2.4 by mid-October and 2.0 by early December, with mean correlation between 0.6 and 0.7 and the minimum spanning tree shrunk by more than a third. Then the recovery is a slow leak: by late 2009 the cloud is back to only five dimensions; by mid-2010, under three again. The scar never fully heals within the window.

That pattern — a stepwise loss of dimension that fails to re-inflate, while prices carry on — is the **disease signature**. The machine watches for it as tripwire T2: effective dimension below 75% of its trailing maximum for three consecutive readings.

## The injury

The animation above is the same twenty assets in 2019–2021. There is no warning in the geometry. Dimension is nine in mid-January 2020, still seven on February 19, and 1.6 by March 17: a 19-trading-day collapse, deeper than Lehman's, with mean correlation peaking near 0.75. Then it climbs back — far more slowly than it fell, as structure always does — and by the vaccine rotation in November the cloud is back to about five dimensions with a directed river running through it; it is wide again by early 2021.

Same bottom. Opposite shape in time. One is a system rotting from the inside; the other is a healthy system hit from the outside.

## Why the VIX can't tell them apart

At weekly resolution the VIX and the geometry move together — neither leads. But the geometry keeps a memory the VIX discards. In mid-2006 the VIX spiked to the same loudness it would reach in August 2007. The 2006 spike left no structural residue: dimension recovered fully. The 2007 spike left a permanent depression. Measured a year on, the VIX had healed 87% of the way; the geometry, 69%. The VIX is alarm loudness. The geometry, we think, is closer to whether the building cracked — on the two episodes we have, which is a sample of one pair.

## What this means for a reader

A falling dimension is not by itself a crash signal. Our hypothesis — the one T2 is built to test, and the one with exactly one confirming case — is that a falling dimension *that keeps failing to recover* is the thing worth watching. And the level of fear around it tells you almost nothing about which kind you're in. This is why the Observatory publishes the whole history of readings rather than a single number, and why the first law on the last page of this guide is about weather.

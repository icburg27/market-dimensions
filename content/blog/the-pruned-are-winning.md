On August 25 we assigned a thousand pretend dollars, on paper, across fifteen stocks and wrote down, in advance, what we expected each group to do. On August 29 those expectations became nine registered claims in a public ledger with due dates and named graders. This is the first time we've looked, and the first thing to report is that one of our clearest calls is losing.

## What we said

The theory divides the market's growth front into layers. The **AI pocket** — NVDA and AMD — is where the organism has been feeding. The **feeding ground** is the supply seam the frontier detector flagged as moving with the pocket, before we had any price evidence that mattered: TSM, VRT, AVGO, ANET, ETN. The **plumbing** is JPMorgan, an artery, there to show what money looks like when it isn't chasing anything. And two names, **VST and CEG**, were connected to the pocket on the detector but had been pruned by price — down 14 to 21 percent on the year when the experiment opened. Claim EXP-5 said the pruning would hold: they would keep underperforming the basket.

## What happened

Through September 4, using the machine's own panel. **Paper experiment — pretend dollars, no real positions.**

| Core-20 body | the vitals basket | −0.5% |

The pruned names are outrunning the feeding ground by nearly four points and the whole body by eight. That is against EXP-5's direction. The pocket keeping pace with its supply chain leans against EXP-3, which said the pocket would decelerate relative to the seam. And the 356.69 pair — AVGO and JPM, which entered at the identical price — sits at 0.998, a hair below parity, against PAIR-1's prediction that the ratio rises in sunlight.

## Why this is on the front page

Eight trading days is weather, not climate. None of these claims is due until February, after six monthly readings, and nothing above is a grade. If we were selling a signal we would wait for a better week to write the first letter.

We're not selling a signal. We're running an instrument in public to find out whether a theory is true, and the theory is only worth anything if its failures are as visible as its successes. So the rule of the house is that grades are append-only, disputes go beside them, and early tension gets published the week it appears — not in February with a story attached.

There is also a more interesting reason. The detector said VST and CEG were becoming frontier tissue. Price said they were pruned. Those two statements disagreed, and we sided with price. If the geometry keeps winning that argument, it means something specific: that correlation structure was carrying information about where capital was about to go that the prices had not yet absorbed — which is the entire thesis of this site, arriving in the least flattering way possible, by proving one of our own calls wrong.

We would take that outcome.

## What we're watching

The vitals as of September 3: effective dimension 8.79, down from 9.8 in mid-August but still wide; mean correlation 0.067; vorticity 0.836 and declining, meaning the Tech-and-Energy river is still running; VIX 14.5, sunlight. No tripwires. This is the state in which the frontier detector can see, and the state in which our claims are supposed to work. If they don't work here, they don't work.

The next weekly letter goes out Saturday; the full monthly reading is the last Saturday of September. The machine drafts; I write the verdict; you get both. And if the pruned are still winning, you'll read it here first.

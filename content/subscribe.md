## What you'll get

**The weekly reading, every Saturday.** The machine finishes its metrics run Saturday morning; by afternoon you have a short letter: what the constellation did this week, which stars drifted toward which, whether the room got wider or tighter — in plain language, with one picture worth keeping. Five minutes. Curiosity, not homework.

**The monthly reading, on the last Saturday.** The full physical: the machine's vitals card and draft report, followed by the human verdict — what the numbers mean, what changed, what we're watching, and how every registered prediction is tracking. Published after the monthly issue in the repository is resolved, so what you read is what was signed off.

**Tripwire notes.** If vorticity crosses 0.92, if effective dimension shows the 2007 disease signature, or if the cloud starts fusing, you get a note within the week. We expect these to be rare. That is what makes them worth sending.

**The grades, always.** Every letter carries the current state of the ledger, including the predictions going the wrong way. Wonder in front, receipts behind.

## What you won't get

Daily commentary. Trade ideas. Anything time-sensitive enough to act on. The whole thesis is that the geometry goes blind during weather; a newsletter that pinged you during weather would be arguing against itself.

*The Reading is research and education, not investment advice. Free. Unsubscribe with one click.*

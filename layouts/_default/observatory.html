How to read the strip
Effective dimension
Participation ratio of the correlation eigenvalues — how many independent directions the twenty stocks actually move in. ~9–10 is wide and differentiated. 2–3 is a fear huddle. Watch the trajectory: slow decay without re-inflation is the 2007 signature.
Mean correlation
Average pairwise ρ over 60 trading days. Tissue tightness. 0.05 is loose; 0.6–0.7 is late 2008.
Vorticity
Share of capital flow that is circulation rather than directed migration (Hodge decomposition of sector-share lead–lag). Below ~0.9 a river is running; above 0.92 the migration is complete and dissolving into rotation.
Separation
Between-cluster ÷ within-cluster distance. Around 1.2 is structured pluralism. Above 1.4 while dimension falls is fusion — the monolith-plus-outcasts shape of a crash.
VIX
Alarm loudness. The geometry remembers what the VIX forgets.
356.69 pair
AVGO ÷ JPM. Both closed at $356.69 on 2026-08-25: a feeding-ground stock and an artery, at parity by accident. A ratio above 1 means the frontier is outrunning the plumbing. Tracked for the ledger's paper claims PAIR-1/PAIR-2; no position exists.

Tripwires

T1 · Migration complete

Vorticity > 0.92. The directed river (Tech and Energy in; Consumer out) has finished and the field has dissolved back into rotation. Not a crash signal — a "the move you were watching is over" signal.

T2 · Disease signature

Effective dimension below 75% of its trailing maximum for three consecutive readings. This is what August 2007 looked like, thirteen months before Lehman, while the index made new highs. Needs forty readings of history to compute; armed, fires no earlier than late October 2026.

T3 · Fusion

Separation above 1.4 while dimension is falling. The cloud is not splitting into healthy tribes; it is collapsing into a monolith with a few outcasts.

When a tripwire fires the machine opens an issue in the repo and The Reading sends a note between Saturdays. Current state is read live from data/alerts.json.
